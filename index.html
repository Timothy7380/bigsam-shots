Profile

Creating visuals that matter

Big Sam is a photographer and cinematographer working out of Osogbo, Osun State. Everything he shoots is made on a phone. His work shows that the eye behind the camera matters more than the gear: every frame is composed, lit and edited by hand, and much of it is shot on an iPhone 15 Pro Max.

His portfolio moves between soft, romantic couple stories, sharp street and menswear looks, editorial fashion and milestone portraits like graduations. The style is warm and cinematic, with rich colour, natural light and poses that feel unforced. People come for the pictures and come back because the shoots feel easy.

He is open for personal sessions, couple shoots, brand campaigns and collaborations across Osun State and beyond.
